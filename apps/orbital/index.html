Ergonomic Rationale

A Proven Handshake Grip as the Foundation

Instead of chasing overly complex, unproven shapes, this project builds on a classical vertical mouse silhouette. The familiar handshake posture naturally rotates the forearm to reduce pronation strain compared to flat mice, creating a dependable, comfortable baseline to explore on-device screen interactions.

ESP32 Hardware Upgrade

Cabled Baseline to Smart Wireless

Standard vertical mice are often basic cabled peripherals. By introducing an ESP32 microcontroller into the loop, we convert that reliable optical tracking core into a modern wireless device with Bluetooth LE and Wi-Fi, while evaluating whether keeping a flexible USB-C charging cable option is preferred for zero battery anxiety.

Wireless Freedom (ESP32 BLE / Wi-Fi)

Cordless desk mobility with multi-device Bluetooth LE pairing and over-the-air Wi-Fi firmware tweaks.

Detachable Paracord USB-C Option

Simultaneous charging while working through long coding/design sessions, eliminating battery downtime.

The Real Innovation

Is an On-Mouse Screen Practical?

The main inquiry of this project is discovering what a round OLED screen truly brings to everyday vertical mouse usage. Can glanceable telemetry and DPI readouts speed up workflow? Can 30-second tactile breaks with retro Pong and Snake stretch tired fingers? We are going to evaluate both modes in daily tests.

Productivity & System Diagnostics

Live DPI stage readout, active host profile (PC/Mac/iPad), polling frequency, and battery gauge.

Tactile Micro-Games & Breaks

Quick mini-games (Pong, Snake) and Pomodoro rest indicators to pause repetitive strain during intense sprints.
